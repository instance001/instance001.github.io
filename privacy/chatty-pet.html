Chatty-Pet Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Chatty-Pet is published by Fractal Media Infrastructure (FMI). This version of the app is designed for local-first play and is not built around ads, in-app purchases, account creation, or data-harvesting business models.

Summary

This version of Chatty-Pet does not require an account, does not include advertising, and does not include in-app purchases. Gameplay progress is stored locally on the device so the app can remember world state and related progress between sessions.

Personal data

In this version of Chatty-Pet, Fractal Media Infrastructure does not collect personal data from users through the app for account creation, advertising, analytics, profiling, or cloud gameplay services.

Local save data

Chatty-Pet stores gameplay progress locally on the device. This may include world state, unlocked items, and other in-app progress needed for normal app functionality between sessions.

This local save data stays on the device and is used only to support the app's operation for the user.

Ads, purchases, and monetization

Chatty-Pet does not include:

  • third-party advertising
  • in-app purchases
  • subscription purchase flows
  • paywall-based core access

Tracking, analytics, and data sharing

This version of Chatty-Pet is not built around cross-app tracking, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or surveillance-style behavioral profiling.

Fractal Media Infrastructure does not sell user data through Chatty-Pet.

Chatty-Pet is not intended to share personal user data with third parties as part of its core gameplay experience.

Permissions

For this version, the Android release manifest does not declare runtime device permissions for the app.

If future versions add permissions or external services, this policy should be updated before or alongside that release.

Children and families

Chatty-Pet is designed to present a simple, child-safe interaction surface. Parents and guardians should review the app themselves to decide whether it is appropriate for a particular child.

Changes to this policy

If Chatty-Pet changes in a way that affects privacy expectations, this page will be updated and the revised version will be published before or alongside that release.
